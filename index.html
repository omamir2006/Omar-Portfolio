01 / About Me

Marketing beyond just running ads.

I’m a digital marketer focused on helping brands build sustainable growth rather than chasing short-term results. I look at the bigger picture—from how customers discover a brand, to how they interact with its website, to what finally motivates a purchase.

I’ve worked on projects in footwear, kidswear and e-commerce, gaining practical experience in product positioning, audience understanding, campaign planning and online customer journeys.

My approach is simple: understand the customer, communicate the right value, measure what is happening, then improve the weakest part of the journey.
